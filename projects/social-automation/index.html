01 · Problem

What need does it solve?

Running a content account means making something new every day and posting it at the right hour, and most of that work is routine.

I wanted to see how far the whole loop could be handed to AI: ideas, original content, timing and publishing, with nothing done by hand once it was set up.

02 · Solution

How it works

Each account has a scheduled Claude routine with its own topic and style. On every run it:

  • Picks a topic that has not been covered yet: a home or car cleaning tip, an Excel or Google Sheets trick, or the news since the last post
  • Creates the post from scratch: for Reels the video, an attention-grabbing cover, the caption and hashtags; for X one witty English summary within 280 characters
  • Rotates between several generated music styles so the Reels do not all sound the same
  • Publishes at the slot when each audience is most active, with no approval step

03 · Architecture

How the system fits together

One routine per account runs on a schedule. It writes the post and checks it against the account's format. Reel videos are hosted in a public repository so the publishing service can fetch them, then Windsor.ai posts them to Instagram. The X post goes out through Typefully. Nothing waits for a manual approval.

05 · Challenges

Hard parts and how they were solved

  1. Original content every day

    Each run checks what the account has already posted, so topics do not repeat, and every video, cover and caption is made new for that post.

  2. Getting attention in the first second

    Covers use a short, bold hook and a before-and-after split, and the music rotates between styles so the feed stays fresh.

  3. Working inside free-plan limits

    The X account posts every 2 to 3 days, and each post sums up everything since the last one, so the monthly post limit is never reached.

  4. Publishing with nobody watching

    Videos are hosted where the publishing service can always reach them, and each post is checked before it goes out.

06 · Results

What came out of it

  • Three accounts live since November 2026, posting on their own.
  • One new Reel a day on each Instagram account, at its prime-time hour.
  • A running record of world events on X, written as a short note every few days.
